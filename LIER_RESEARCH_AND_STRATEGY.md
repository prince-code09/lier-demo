## 1. Real-World Luxury Brand & Competitor Research (Phase B)

To position LIER as a premier automotive fragrance house, we conducted an in-depth analysis of global benchmark brands across luxury fragrance, automotive design, and high-conversion e-commerce.

| Brand | Category / URL | Key Strengths & Patterns | What to Adapt for LIER | What NOT to Copy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aesop** | Artisanal Fragrance & Care<br>`https://www.aesop.com` | Minimalist editorial grids, restrained warm alabaster and muted earth tones, literary typography, detailed formulation narratives. | Clean sensory ingredient breakdowns (Top / Heart / Base notes), subtle hover transitions, generous white space, and understated elegance. | Overly stark clinical minimalism; Aesop lacks the automotive dynamism and dark luxury cockpit atmosphere required for LIER. |

## 4. Visual Direction & Design Concept Evaluation (Phase D)

We formulated three distinct aesthetic directions for LIER:

### Concept A: Obsidian Luxury (RECOMMENDED & SELECTED)
- **Palette:** Rich obsidian (`#0A0A0C`), warm charcoal (`#14141A`), metallic champagne gold (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C07B`), crisp alabaster (`#F9F8F6`).
- **Typography:** Refined display serif (*Playfair Display* / *Cormorant*) paired with clean geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **Vibe:** Cinematic, bespoke, tailored cockpit ambience. Complements LIER's dark packaging, metallic gold foil engravings, and warm amber oils flawlessly.

### Concept B: Modern Atelier
- **Palette:** Warm ivory, raw linen, muted antique bronze, soft cream.
- **Vibe:** Parisian perfume boutique. While elegant, it fails to capture the technical excitement and cockpit environment of premium automobiles.

### Concept C: Automotive Performance
- **Palette:** Graphite, brushed titanium silver, electric cyan, carbon black.
- **Vibe:** High-performance supercar track day. While dynamic, it feels too synthetic and sports-oriented, detracting from the organic beech wood and natural fragrance oils.

**Selection:** **Concept A (Obsidian Luxury)** provides the perfect harmony of haute perfumery romance and automotive sophistication.
